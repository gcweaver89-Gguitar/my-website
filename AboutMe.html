About Me

My Experience

I have over 23 years of guitar-playing experience, including more than seven years dedicated to focused music theory study. Like many beginners, I started with dreams of becoming a rockstar and believed I didn’t need to learn music theory—or even the names of the notes I was playing. My approach was simple: “If it sounds good, that’s all that matters.” With time, however, I came to appreciate just how essential a solid foundation in music theory truly is. There’s a saying in music that I’ve found to be especially true: “You have to know the rules to understand when to break them.” You don’t need to follow every rule perfectly, but understanding them provides a reliable framework to support your creativity and growth as a musician.
